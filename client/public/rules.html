Chat Rules

  1. You must be 18 or older. Accounts that appear to belong to minors are removed.
  2. No spam, advertising, links, or promoting other sites, apps or numbers.
  3. No harassment, threats, hate speech or sexual messages to people who did not ask for them.
  4. No scams, asking for money, gift cards or crypto.
  5. No sharing other people's personal information or photos.
  6. No impersonating staff or other users.

Breaking the rules leads to a temporary ban. The ban screen always tells you the reason and when it ends. We never ban by IP address, so other people on your network are not affected.
