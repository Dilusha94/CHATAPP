Privacy Policy

Messages: the messages you send exist only while you are online. They are permanently deleted when you log out, when you are inactive for 5 minutes, or 2 minutes after you close the site.

Sign-in records (kept 1 year): each time you join we record the time, your IP address, your device ID, the nickname, gender, age and country you used, and your browser type. We use this only to stop abuse: when someone is reported, a moderator can see whether the same person keeps coming back under different names, and ban them. These records never contain messages, are visible only to our moderators, are not sold or shared, and are deleted automatically after 1 year. Bans and reports (who reported whom and why, without messages) are kept for the same year.

Reports: if someone reports a conversation, a copy of that one conversation is kept for up to 7 days so a moderator can review it, then deleted.

Device ID: your browser stores a random ID so bans apply to the device that broke the rules, not to everyone sharing your internet connection. We never ban by IP address alone.

Country: your country is detected from your IP address by our network provider (Cloudflare); you can't choose it.

Your rights: to ask what we hold about you, or to have your sign-in records deleted, write to us through the contact page and mention your nickname and the date you used the site. Records needed to deal with an open report or ban may be kept until that is resolved.

Ads: we show ads from Adsterra. Their scripts may use cookies; see the Adsterra privacy policy.

Contact: contact page.
